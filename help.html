Try a recce in a few simple steps.

Use the sample records to explore the field and office experience. Open this link on your phone, tablet or computer.

For the recce person

  1. Select Try the field demo and open Shree Stores → Start recce.
  2. Check the shop address and contact. Allow device location if you want to try it, or continue with the typed address. Type a site note or choose Speak a note.
  3. Continue to Materials & marking. Enter the measurements and choose or type the placement. Add another material card for each physical board.
  4. Capture/upload a photo or use the sample. Select Mark image, draw a shape and select Save marked image. Measurements are entered manually.
  5. Continue to Review & submit. Check the material views and submit. Download a marked PNG from the editor or all marked images as a ZIP from the review screen.

For the office team

Open the office dashboard. Summary cards lead to the matching jobs. Review submitted work, verify it or request corrections. Editable jobs offer Reassign job.

In Reports, filter jobs, choose and order columns, enter a heading, then preview. Download Excel/CSV or choose Print/Save PDF. Selected photo columns are included in print output; spreadsheet exports explain how to obtain image files separately.

Open the office dashboard →

Support and saving

Try typing or recording a support issue. Replies and Pending, Processing and Resolved status are simulated within your browser. No messages are sent to an actual office, developer or WhatsApp number.

Your changes, photos and recordings stay in this browser and are not synchronised with another person. Use Save demo backup if you want to keep a portable copy. The ? button offers backup restore or resetting the sample records.

Camera, microphone and location access depend on the device/browser and your permission. A device file picker may appear instead of a camera. This prototype contains fictional records and no production customer database.
